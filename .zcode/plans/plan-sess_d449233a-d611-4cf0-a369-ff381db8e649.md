## 目标

设置页各选项的标题、副标题字重过重(M3 `titleMedium`/`labelMedium` 默认 w500),统一降为 `FontWeight.w400`(常规字重),保留字号不变,与 Pixel OS 设置项常规字重风格一致。

## 改动清单

全部在现有样式基础上加 `fontWeight: FontWeight.w400`(无样式结构变化):

### 设置项核心组件(主要受影响处)
1. `lib/pages/setting/widgets/normal_item.dart`
   - 标题:`theme.textTheme.titleMedium!` → `copyWith(fontWeight: FontWeight.w400)`
   - 副标题:`labelMedium.copyWith(...)` 中追加 `fontWeight: FontWeight.w400`
2. `lib/pages/setting/widgets/switch_item.dart`
   - 标题:`titleMedium.copyWith(...)` 中追加 `fontWeight: FontWeight.w400`
   - 副标题:`labelMedium.copyWith(...)` 中追加 `fontWeight: FontWeight.w400`
3. `lib/pages/setting/view.dart`(设置主页列表项)
   - `titleStyle`:`titleMedium!` → `copyWith(fontWeight: FontWeight.w400)`
   - `subTitleStyle`:`labelMedium.copyWith(...)` 中追加 `fontWeight: FontWeight.w400`

### 设置子页/其他选项文字(保持体系一致)
4. `lib/pages/setting/pages/color_select.dart`(主题选择页)
   - `titleStyle` / `subTitleStyle` 同样追加 `fontWeight: FontWeight.w400`
5. `lib/pages/setting/pages/play_speed_set.dart:246`('倍速列表'区块标题)
6. `lib/pages/setting/models/privacy_settings.dart:61`(账号模式详情小标题)
7. 选择类对话框选项文字:
   - `lib/pages/setting/widgets/select_dialog.dart:33,59`
   - `lib/pages/setting/widgets/multi_select_dialog.dart:50`
   - `lib/pages/setting/widgets/ordered_multi_select_dialog.dart:53`

## 验证
- `flutter analyze` 无错误
- 说明:`copyWith(fontWeight: w400)` 在全局字重设置(appFontWeight)之上显式覆盖,设置项字重固定为常规,不受全局粗体设置影响
